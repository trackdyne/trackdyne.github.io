## 1. GENERAL INFORMATION ABOUT THE PRODUCT

The underwater acoustic modems of the **Vexa** family are designed for:
- transmission of arbitrary digital user data through the water column;
- estimating the propagation time of an acoustic signal between two modems;

The Vexa Mini, Vexa Max and Vexa Locator modems have a built-in pressure and temperature sensor, which allows using them for:
- estimating the local immersion depth, pressure, ambient temperature and supply voltage;

- The Vexa Mini, Vexa Mini LT, Vexa Max and Vexa Max OEM modems have a built-in supply voltage measurement circuit;
- The Vexa Locator modems have a phased array antenna and can be used to determine the horizontal angle of arrival of the signal from other modems of the **Vexa** family.

The modems (except Vexa Max OEM) are made as a monoblock on a cable, completely potted in polyurethane compound, and are designed for
operation in fresh and salt water (salinity level up to **38 PSU**) and in the temperature range **from -5 to +50 °C**.
The maximum operating hydrostatic pressure is **30 bar**, which approximately corresponds to 300 meters of water column. The device is powered by a DC source with a voltage of **5 .. 12 V**. The power consumption is **0.33 and 6 W** in receive and transmit modes, respectively, for Vexa Mini devices and **0.33 and 15 W** in receive and transmit modes, respectively, for all other devices of the family. Interfacing with the control system is performed via the **UART 9600** interface.  
The data rate over the underwater acoustic channel is **78 bit/s**.

## 3. MANUFACTURER'S WARRANTY. SERVICE LIFE
The warranty period for the supplied equipment kit is **24 (twenty-four) months** and is counted from the date of actual receipt of the devices by the customer, unless otherwise provided by the contract.
If, during the warranty period, defects or latent defects are found in the product (latent defects are defects that could not be detected during a check usual for the given product and were revealed only during operation), the Supplier shall eliminate them at its own expense by repairing the product or replacing it with a new one within a period of **no more than 30 (thirty) calendar days** and specified in the certificate of transfer of the product for repair.
The warranty period for the product is extended by the time during which the product was not used because of the identified defects. The time during which the product was not used is counted from the date of the written notification to the Supplier of the identified defects until the day of receipt of the product under the acceptance certificate after repair (replacement).
When the product is replaced, the warranty period for the product is counted anew from the date the parties sign the acceptance certificate after repair (replacement).
Delivery of the product for replacement work from the Customer's premises, and after the work has been completed to the Customer's warehouse, is carried out using the Customer's own resources.

The warranty obligations do not apply to the product in the case of:  
- Violation of the rules for operating the product;
- Electrical damage to the product caused by water ingress through the free end of the cable, which resulted in its failure and occurred after it was handed over to the Customer;
- Mechanical damage to the product that caused its loss or failure and occurred after it was handed over to the Customer.

## 4. NOTES ON OPERATION AND STORAGE

### 4.1. Interchangeability
The devices are fully interchangeable electrically within the same model.

### 4.2. Special storage requirements
The use of organic solvents and/or aggressive acids and/or alkalis to remove contamination from the surface of the modems and their cable is not allowed.

### 4.3 Special safety precautions during operation
The devices are not rescue or human life-support equipment.
Any work using the modems must be performed in strict compliance with the requirements of the Intersectoral rules for diving/underwater operations, as well as the departmental and local regulations in force for the Customer governing diving operations.
