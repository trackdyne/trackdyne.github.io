## KEY FEATURES

* **Minimal size and weight**
* **Impact-resistant design, IP67 with the lid closed**
* **Long-life built-in LiFePO4 power supply**
* **Can be powered from an external power source**
* **3 mm thick stainless steel faceplate**

## DESCRIPTION

The VigilArc Deck device combines the functions of an autonomous power supply for the direction-finding stations [VigilArc Array](/documentation/EN/VigilArc/VigilArc_Array_Specification_en.html) and a switching hub for interfacing the station with the user's PC, as well as for connecting sources of heading and position data for the direction-finding station. It contains protection circuits against reverse polarity and overvoltage for the connected devices.

The unit is housed in a small impact-resistant plastic case. It has a front panel made of high-quality stainless steel. The modern lithium iron phosphate batteries used in the unit provide operation at sub-zero temperatures and **more than a thousand** charge-discharge cycles. If necessary, the unit can be operated from an external 12 V DC source while the built-in battery is charged from the standard charger.
