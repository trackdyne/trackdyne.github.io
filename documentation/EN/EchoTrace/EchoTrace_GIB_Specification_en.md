## KEY FEATURES

* **High-performance combined GPS/GLONASS receiver**
* **Rugged, durable, easily noticeable housing**
* **Automatic activation in water**
* **Long battery life of up to 24 hours**
* **Reliable and noise-resistant digital broadband underwater acoustic communication technology**

## DESCRIPTION

The **EchoTrace GIB** navigation sonobuoy of the **[EchoTrace](/documentation/EN/EchoTrace/EchoTrace_DataBrief_en.html)** system, in a set of four such devices, forms a floating long navigation base that supports positioning of the **[EchoTrace Pinger](/documentation/EN/EchoTrace/EchoTrace_Pinger_Specification_en.html)** pinger or the **SubVox** diver telephone station.  
The device has a float-and-spar (spar buoy) design and has no air cavities or controls: it switches on automatically when immersed in water, and switches off automatically when the buoy is taken out of the water.
